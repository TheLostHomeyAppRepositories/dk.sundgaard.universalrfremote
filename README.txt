Control the 433 MHz remotes Homey can't learn on its own, such as those for awnings, blinds, sockets and gates.
Add a remote with your own buttons and give each one its code by letting Homey listen to the remote, from a Flipper Zero recording or by typing it in.

Give the buttons a function such as Up, Stop and Down or On and Off, and the device gets Homey's own controls, works with voice assistants and can move an awning to a position.
Remotes that change their code on every press, such as Somfy, can't be copied.
